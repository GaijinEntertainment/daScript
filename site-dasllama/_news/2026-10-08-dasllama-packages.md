title: dasllama installs with one command now - brew, scoop, pip, a .deb, an .rpm - with the 0.6.5 release candidate.
---

The `v0.6.5-RC1` [release](https://github.com/GaijinEntertainment/daScript/releases/tag/v0.6.5-RC1)
ships dasllama as packages beside the daslang SDK for the first time: a Homebrew cask (the Mac
app) and formula (the commands), a scoop manifest for Windows, `.deb` and `.rpm` packages for
amd64 and arm64, Python wheels for every platform, and the per-platform archives. Every form
carries the same four programs: `dasllama-server`, `dasllama-cli`, `dasllama-bench` and the
watchdog. The commands are in the [get it](/#get-it) section above; a candidate's wheels live on
test.pypi until the release.
