dasllama now releases on its own tags, `dasllama-vX.Y.Z`, apart from the daslang SDK's: an
engine fix reaches brew, scoop, pip, the `.deb` and the `.rpm` without waiting for a daslang
release. The first one, [dasllama-v0.6.6-RC1](https://github.com/GaijinEntertainment/daScript/releases/tag/dasllama-v0.6.6-RC1),
carries the re-minted arm-neon, x86-vnni512 and x86-avx2 class profiles and the shared vision
kernels; the [get it](/#get-it) section names every form, and the rolling `dasllama-server`
archives are refreshed from it.
