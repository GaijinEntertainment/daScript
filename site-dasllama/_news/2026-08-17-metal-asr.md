title: Metal support lands in speech-to-text — whisper large-v3-turbo at 49x realtime on an M1 Max.
---

ASR now runs on the GPU: encoder, cross-attention and decoder all serve on Metal, on by
default on Apple GPUs. On an M1 Max, whisper large-v3-turbo transcribes a 3:19 clip in
4.0 s — 49x realtime, against 36x for whisper.cpp's Metal path and 12x for our own CPU
lane. The [ladder](ladder.html) now splits ASR into the same cpu / cpu + accel / gpu
categories the LLM board has, each paired against the reference's matching mode.
